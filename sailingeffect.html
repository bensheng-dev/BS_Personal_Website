The Sailing Effect

Skills: StarCCM+, CFD Analysis

July 2026 - August 2026

Solar cars are built to minimize drag, but a crosswind hits the car's flank at an angle — much like wind filling a sail. This project investigates whether that "sailing effect" can be harnessed for useful thrust instead of just fought as drag. Here are some targets for this project:

1. Quantify the thrust generated by crosswind interaction with the car body

2. Compare one-sail and two-sail configurations

3. Validate results with high-fidelity StarCCM+ simulations

Overview

Using StarCCM+, I ran 3D crosswind simulations with 25 prism layers and over 14 million cells to study how UBC Solar's car interacts with wind hitting it from the side — effectively acting like a sail.

I tested both a one-sail and two-sail configuration to compare the aerodynamic benefits of each, running more than 15 simulations in total.

The best configuration achieved over 100 newtons of thrust, showing that crosswinds can meaningfully contribute to — or work against — the car's performance depending on the setup.
